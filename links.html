Links & further reading

No hyperlinked webring here — this shrine would rather not guess at addresses for museums and archives that might move or paywall between now and whenever you’re reading this. What follows is a plain bibliography instead: real institutions and books, worth searching for directly rather than trusting a decade-old link. The reference panel that rides along on every page of this shrine carries the short version of it.

Reading, if the biography page left you wanting more

  • Any general survey of twentieth-century Chinese painting will place Qi Baishi alongside contemporaries like Xu Beihong and Huang Binhong — useful for seeing what made his plain, popular subjects unusual next to the literati mainstream of his day.
  • Exhibition catalogues from the collections in the panel, where available, tend to reproduce the brushwork at a scale a general biography can’t.
  • For the three works reproduced on the gallery page, the source and licence of each photograph is printed under it — those are the only images on this site we could establish clear rights for.
